What Is HVAC & Air Duct Sanitization?

Your heating and cooling system pushes air through every room of your home. Over time — or in a matter of weeks during construction — dust and debris collect inside the ductwork and get blown back into your living space.

Our service does two things. First, we physically remove the dust and buildup from your ducts, vents and returns. Then we fog HOCl through the entire system so it starts clean. The result is a system that is clean from the air handler to the last register.

What We Cover

  • Supply ducts and vents in every room
  • Return air ducts and grilles
  • Air handler and blower area
  • Coils, cleaned when needed
  • Registers and diffusers
  • HOCl fogging through the whole system

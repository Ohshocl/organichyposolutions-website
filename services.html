How Our Service Works

The same four-step process across every service vertical — assessment, scheduling, treatment, documentation

1
Assessment

We evaluate your facility type, square footage, pathogen concerns, scheduling constraints, and any certification requirements — organic, FSIS, or otherwise — to build the right treatment protocol.

2
Scheduling

We work around your operation — evening, overnight, weekend, or early morning. Residential families can schedule around school and work. Commercial facilities schedule around occupancy. Agricultural operations schedule around production cycles.

3
HOCl Treatment

Professional fogging or spray application reaches every surface — HVAC zones, equipment crevices, fabric fibers, ceiling surfaces, and areas inaccessible to spray-and-wipe methods.

4
Documentation

Service records provided with every treatment — EPA registration number, USDA Organic certification, application areas, date and time. Suitable for FSIS files, organic certification records, compliance documentation, and insurance files.

Our Story: Revolutionizing Sanitization

Founded by Brandon O'Neal and Bob Strang, Organic HypoSolutions (OHSHOCL) emerged from a simple yet powerful vision: bringing hypochlorous acid, the same molecule your immune system makes, to every business and home in Utah.

We discovered that your own immune system produces hypochlorous acid (HOCl). This revelation led us to pioneer commercial HOCl sanitization services that are:

  • An EPA-registered hospital disinfectant
  • Mild, with no harsh fumes
  • Backed by a separate USDA Certified Organic cleaning line

As a Utah HOCl provider with two certified product lines, we're not just another disinfectant company - we're health advocates bringing medical-grade sanitization to everyday spaces.

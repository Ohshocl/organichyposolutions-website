About Organic HypoSolutions

Utah's only USDA Certified Organic mold remediation post-treatment provider and comprehensive sanitization company

Our Mission

Founded by Brandon O'Neal and Bob Strang, Organic HypoSolutions delivers professional HOCl sanitization and mold remediation post-treatment services to homeowners, food processing facilities, healthcare institutions, agricultural operations, and residential properties across Utah. Our products come in two certified lines: USDA Certified Organic (ships nationwide) and EPA Registered (Utah-only disinfectants). Our mission is to provide the safest, most effective treatment available while protecting people, animals, and the environment.

Comprehensive Expertise

From post-remediation mold treatment and food processing protocols to healthcare-grade disinfection and agricultural biosecurity, we utilize advanced hypochlorous acid technology and specialized application methods tailored to each situation's unique requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HOCl?

HOCl is the same molecule the human immune system produces. Most treated spaces are ready to re-enter in about two hours.

Do new homes need their air ducts sanitized?

Construction leaves drywall dust and debris in ductwork. Sanitizing before move-in means the system starts clean.
